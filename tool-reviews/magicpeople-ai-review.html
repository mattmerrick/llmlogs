What is Magic People AI Resume Screener?

Magic People AI Resume Screener is an AI-powered tool designed specifically for HR professionals and recruiters to automate the tedious process of resume screening. The platform uses advanced AI to analyze resumes in seconds, extract key information, and rank candidates by fit - allowing recruiters to focus on the best prospects rather than manual paperwork.

The tool addresses the critical pain points in modern hiring: time waste, unconscious bias, and the high cost of bad hires. With over 1,000 HR professionals already using the platform, it's proven to transform hiring workflows from hours of manual work to minutes of AI-powered analysis.

How It Works

1. Upload Resumes

Simply drag and drop PDF, DOCX, or ZIP files containing resumes. The platform can handle individual files or bulk uploads of hundreds of resumes at once.

2. AI Analysis

The AI reads and extracts key information from each resume, parsing skills, experience, qualifications, and other relevant details automatically.

3. Get Ranked Results

Receive ranked candidates with fit scores, allowing you to immediately identify the best prospects and focus your time on qualified candidates.
